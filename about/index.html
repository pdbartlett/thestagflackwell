Who we are

Traditional village pub

Perched in the heart of Flackwell Heath with sweeping views across the Wye Valley, The Stag is a traditional village pub that has been serving the local community since the mid-19th century. Perfectly located just minutes from J3 of the M40 and near the River Thames it's the ideal spot to gather over great local ales and seasonal British dishes, including our classic Sunday roasts. Whether you're dropping in after a countryside stroll or meeting with family, come for the views and stay for the warm hospitality.

Our Philosophy

We believe that a great pub is the anchor of its community, by providing the best that local produce has to offer, alongside great drinks and friendly service. Pop in for a pint, or take a look at our menus.

We want to be the pub that's there for you whatever the weather and occasion, whether it's cold beer on a sunny afternoon in the garden, or a cosy winter meal, our team is passionate about creating a welcoming atmosphere where everyone feels at home.

Our History

  • 18th Century Roots: began as a mid-1700s Chiltern timber and brick building before taking on a pub license around 1850 to serve the local agricultural and mill-working community.
  • Interior Character: renowned locally for retaining traditional period features—including original timber beams, flagstones, and a central open fireplace—earning it a place on CAMRA's historic pub interior listings.
  • Village Anchor: positioned on Heath End Road, it historically served as an informal gathering spot during the village's famous summer cherry harvest and for workers from the paper mills in the valley below.

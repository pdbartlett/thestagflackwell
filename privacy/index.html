Our Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled responsibly. This privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you interact with The Stag pub and restaurant.

Privacy Policy

  1. The Stag pub and restaurant is operated by Kentigern's Nag Ltd. (Company Registration Number: 17506484)
  2. We collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number and any notes you provide when you make a reservation.
  3. If you wish you can sign up for our newsletter by providing your name and email address.
  4. Your data is used solely for the purpose of providing you with our services and optionally keeping you informed about our events, menu changes and other news.
  5. Your personal information is removed from our booking systems after a period of 12 months.
  6. If you unsubscribe from our newsletter, your information will be removed from our mailing list within 30 days.
  7. We do not share your personal information with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.
  8. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
  9. You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal information at any time. Please contact manager@thestagflackwell.co.uk for details.

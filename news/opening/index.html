New Owners & The Return of the Roast

Join us for Sunday lunch on 22 November to celebrate our refurbishment.

We’re excited to announce that The Stag is officially opening its doors under new management on Sunday 22nd November, and we can’t wait to welcome you all in.

For those who don’t know us yet, we’re Dan and Sophie:

In the Kitchen: Dan brings years of head chef experience from local award-winning pubs, serving up proper, quality British pub food.

Out Front: Sophie brings a similar background of award-winning locals, focusing on friendly service and a welcoming atmosphere.

And most importantly: Sunday Roasts Are Back!

We’re launching on a Sunday for one main reason: bringing back the traditional Sunday Roast. It’s been a while since The Stag offered roasts, so we’re excited to share our take on the classic Sunday lunch with all the traditional sides.
